Before you send

Questions we get asked first.

How quickly can you visit the site?

Usually within three to five working days for sites within the Nairobi area, and within two weeks further afield. Urgent visits can often be arranged sooner if plant is already working nearby.

Do I need drawings before contacting you?

No. Plenty of enquiries start with a plot number and an idea. If the project needs a design, we can tell you what has to be produced and by whom before any pricing is meaningful.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. A single driveway, a soil test or a boundary survey are all normal enquiries. Small work is often how longer relationships start.

Can you take a project over midway through?

Sometimes. We would survey what has been built, test where necessary and give you an honest assessment before agreeing to continue. We will not take responsibility for work we have not inspected.

How do you price work?

Measured against a bill of quantities where one exists, or against our own take-off from the drawings and site survey. Provisional sums are used only where the scope genuinely cannot be fixed yet, and they are flagged clearly.

What happens after handover?

You receive as-built drawings, test records and the snagging sign-off as a single pack. Defects liability terms are set out in the contract and we return to close out anything raised within that period.

Prefer to just call?

The office picks up during working hours, and whoever answers can usually tell you straight away whether we are the right fit.
