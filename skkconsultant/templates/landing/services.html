Service 01

Excavation & earthworks

Bulk cut and fill, trenching, basement digs and site clearance. Our own machines and operators, so mobilisation does not wait on a hire yard.

We plan the earthworks around the material balance first. Moving spoil twice is the most common hidden cost on a project, so we set the haul routes and stockpile locations before anything tracks onto site.

What this covers

  • Site clearance and topsoil strip
  • Bulk cut and fill to formation level
  • Basement and foundation excavation
  • Service and drainage trenching
  • Rock breaking and removal
  • Compaction and platform testing
Get an excavation quote

Service 02

Cabro & paving

Interlocking blocks, kerbs, channels and edge restraints for driveways, walkways, yards and commercial parking areas.

Most failed cabro is a base problem, not a block problem. We build the sub-base to the loading it will actually see, get the falls right so water leaves the surface, and restrain every edge — which is why our yards are still flat after several rainy seasons.

What this covers

  • Sub-base design and compaction
  • Interlocking cabro to pattern
  • Kerbs, channels and edge restraint
  • Heavy-duty yard and parking areas
  • Walkways and pedestrian surfaces
  • Surface falls and drainage tie-in
Get a paving quote

Service 03

Site surveying

Topographic survey, boundary verification, setting out, level control and as-built records — in-house, on our own programme.

An error in the setting out multiplies through every trade that follows it. Our survey team establishes the control network before mobilisation and re-checks it at each major stage, so the frame lands where the drawings say it should.

What this covers

  • Topographic and contour survey
  • Control network establishment
  • Setting out for structures and roads
  • Level and verticality checks
  • Volume calculations for earthworks
  • As-built drawings and handover records
Book a site survey

Service 04

Soil testing & site investigation

Trial pits, sampling, classification and bearing capacity assessment — so foundations get designed against real ground, not assumed ground.

Investigation is the cheapest week on any project. A soft layer found in a trial pit costs a foundation redesign; the same layer found after the slab is poured costs the building. We report what we find plainly, including when it is inconvenient.

What this covers

  • Trial pits and sample recovery
  • Soil classification and profiling
  • Bearing capacity assessment
  • Compaction and density testing
  • Groundwater observation
  • Written report with foundation guidance
Request a site investigation

Service 05

Building construction

Foundations, reinforced concrete frames, blockwork, roofing and finishes for residential, commercial and institutional buildings.

We run building work as a sequence of hold points rather than a race to the roof. Reinforcement is inspected before every pour and the following trade only starts once the previous one is signed off — which is what keeps the finishes clean at the end.

What this covers

  • Strip, pad and raft foundations
  • Reinforced concrete frames and slabs
  • Blockwork, brickwork and partitions
  • Structural steel erection
  • Roofing and external envelope
  • Internal finishes and snagging
Discuss a build

Service 06

Road construction & drainage

Access roads, estate roads and haul routes — formation, sub-base, base course, surfacing and the drainage that keeps them serviceable.

A road fails at its edges and its drainage long before the surface wears out. We build the shoulders and the storm channels as part of the same package, so water leaves the carriageway instead of sitting under it.

What this covers

  • Road formation and subgrade preparation
  • Sub-base and base course laying
  • Surfacing and shoulder construction
  • Storm channels and side drains
  • Culverts and cross drainage
  • Manholes, gullies and outfalls
Get a roads quote
